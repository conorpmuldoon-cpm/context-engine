Hudson Franz scored on a three-point play and recorded a steal in the final 17 seconds to propel the University of Maine to a 90-86 overtime victory over Le Moyne College on Friday afternoon at the Kirby Sports Center in Easton, Pennsylvania. The Black Bears’ Franz finished with 12 points, three rebounds, three steals and two assists in the opening day contest of The Lafayette Classic. His conventional three-point play with 17 seconds remaining gave Maine an 88-86 lead, and he sealed the win by intercepting a cross-court pass with 11 seconds left and feeding Oliver Faubert for a layup.
