DPW Announces Road Reconstruction Project on North Geddes Street

Published on November 02, 2026

Syracuse, N.Y. – The Department of Public Works announced the continuation of a road reconstruction project on North Geddes Street, Tuesday, November 3 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The project will include paving North Geddes Street between Spencer and West Genesee Street. During construction, travel lanes will remain open with flaggers alternating the flow of vehicles through a single open lane.

 

On Wednesday, November 4, paving will continue on North Geddes Street from West Genesee Street to Erie Boulevard. While road construction is in progress with flaggers alternating the flow of vehicles through a single open lane. Drivers may experience delays and are advised to use alternate routes if possible. Construction is expected to be completed within 48 hours; actual timeframe is contingent on favorable weather.  

 

For Media Inquiries

For media inquiries, members of the press should contact the Office of Communications by emailing press@syr.gov.
