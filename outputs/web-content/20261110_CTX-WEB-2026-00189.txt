WATER CREWS WORKING TO REPAIR A WATER MAIN BREAK ON LAFORTE AVENUE

Published on November 10, 2026

SYRACUSE, N.Y. — The Syracuse Department of Water is working to repair a 36-inch water main break at the intersection of Laforte Avenue and Arthur Street. Households and businesses in the area may experience water service disruptions. Residents and businesses across the City may also experience low water pressure while repairs are underway. Arthur Street between Laforte Avenue and Grant Avenue will be closed to all vehicle traffic until repair work is completed. Sidewalks remain open and traffic detours are in place. The repair is expected to be completed in approximately three to five hours. Residents and businesses with questions or concerns can contact the Department of Water at 315-448-8360.

 

For Media Inquiries
